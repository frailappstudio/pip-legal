Pip: Focus Pet

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

The short version. Pip never sees what you watch or type, only how long, and your screen time never leaves your phone. There are no ads, no account, and we don't sell anything.

Pip is made by Frail App Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Pip app does with information.

Screen Time

Pip uses Apple's Screen Time features to learn how long you spend in the apps and categories you choose. Apple gives Pip your choices as private tokens, so Pip cannot see the names of your apps or what you do in them. The minutes are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your Pip

Pip's name, mood, stage, sun drops, outfits and your daily budget are saved on your iPhone. A copy is saved to your private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit) so your Pip can come back if you reinstall. That copy lives in your own iCloud account. We cannot read it.

Purchases

Pip+ purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your Apple ID has Pip+. RevenueCat receives purchase information and a random app user ID, not your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Analytics

Pip uses Google's Firebase Analytics to count things like which screens are opened, whether onboarding is finished and whether the paywall is seen. Events contain no screen time minutes and no app names. Firebase also collects basic device information such as phone model, iOS version and a random identifier for the app install. Pip does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites. See Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow them, Pip sends reminders from your phone, for example when Pip gets foggy. These are created on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no ad networks.
  • No selling or renting of information.
  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers collected.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Deleting the app removes what is stored on your phone.
  • To remove your iCloud backup, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Pip, and delete its data.
  • To ask about analytics data, email us and we'll help.

Children

Pip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and for big changes we'll tell you in the app.
