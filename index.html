Pip: Focus Pet

Support

Pip is a small creature that lives on the time you spend off your social feeds.

Need help? Email frailappstudio@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We read every message.

Common questions

Pip shows 0 minutes. Pip updates after you use one of your chosen apps for a few minutes. Open Me > Tracked apps to check your picks, and make sure Screen Time access is on in Settings.

How do I change my apps? Me > Tracked apps.

How do I cancel Pip+? Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Pip. Cancelling keeps Pip+ until the end of the period you paid for.

I reinstalled and my Pip is gone. Sign in to the same iCloud account. The first screen offers "Welcome back".

How does billing work? Pip+ is billed through your Apple ID. Payment is charged at confirmation, or after the free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.
